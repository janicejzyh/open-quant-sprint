Track 03 Part 1: causal BTC/ETH leader-flow impulse


Implementation
Added leader_flow_impulse(df, span=12) in src/contrib/janice.py. At each
hour, it sums BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT signed quote flow, divides by their
combined quote volume, and smooths the resulting hourly series with a
causal EWM. It broadcasts the result
to every symbol while preserving the input index and row order.
